🎮 GAME MECHANICS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Energy System:
  • Starting energy: 1000
  • Recharge rate: 1 energy/second (upgradeable)
  • Cost per tap: 1 energy (scales with upgrades)

Upgrades:
  🔋 Energy Limit    - +50% max energy    (Base: 100 💎, x1.8 multiplier)
  ⚡ Tap Power       - +50% per tap       (Base: 50 💎, x1.6 multiplier)
  🤖 Auto Farm       - +10 coins/hour     (Base: 500 💎, x2.0 multiplier)
  ⚡ Recharge Speed  - +50% recharge      (Base: 200 💎, x1.7 multiplier)

Achievements:
  👆 First Tap       - Make 1 tap         (Reward: 10 💎)
  🔥 Tapper          - Make 100 taps      (Reward: 50 💎)
  ⚡ Pro Tapper      - Make 1000 taps     (Reward: 200 💎)
  💰 First K         - Earn 1000 coins    (Reward: 100 💎)
  💎 Rich            - Earn 10000 coins   (Reward: 500 💎)
  🏆 Level 5         - Reach level 5      (Reward: 250 💎)
  👑 Level 10        - Reach level 10     (Reward: 1000 💎)

Level Formula:
  level = floor(log10(balance + 1) * 2) + 1
